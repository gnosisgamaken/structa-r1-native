Exactly 20 captures: 7 screen targets + 7 real spaces + 6 materials/objects. Use S1–S7 from this screen or print them. Fill the R1 camera frame with only the cream test area, then use your surroundings for P1–P7 and M1–M6.

real spaces

P1–P7
P1 · room overviewStand in a doorway and capture most of a bedroom, office, or living room.
P2 · circulationFrame a doorway with the connected corridor or next room visible beyond it.
P3 · exteriorCapture a courtyard, balcony, façade, or quiet street without identifiable people.
P4 · low lightUse the same room with the main light off; keep enough shape to judge uncertainty.
P5 · reflectionCapture a mirror, glazed door, or reflective appliance showing layered space.
P6 · obstructionFrame a chair, doorway, or table partly hidden by a bag, blanket, or open door.
P7 · no scaleCapture a tight architectural corner with no hand, furniture, ruler, or familiar object.

materials + objects

M1–M6
M1 · material close-upFill the frame with wood, stone, woven fabric, tile, or cardboard.
M2 · assemblyCapture a chair joint, cabinet hinge, lamp connection, or visible fastener.
M3 · damageUse a harmless scratch, chipped edge, loose thread, dent, or small surface crack.
M4 · object groupArrange four to six ordinary objects together on a desk or table.
M5 · fine textureMove close to a textile weave, paper fibres, brushed metal, or wood grain.
M6 · ambiguous materialCrop painted metal, silicone, plastic, or laminate so its object context disappears.

capture rules

  • Use only synthetic/non-client material and avoid faces, addresses, screens, and personal documents.
  • Store exactly one original and wait for its reading or explicit unavailable/degraded state before the next target.
  • A schema-valid “visual signal insufficient” result is transport-valid, not degraded. It is a semantic non-match for clear targets S1–S6, P1–P6, and M1–M5.
  • For deliberately ambiguous controls S7, P7, and M6, appropriate uncertainty may be a semantic match if it avoids a confident unsupported claim.
  • Record: ID · transport valid/degraded · semantic matched/non-match · clipped yes/no · silent yes/no.
  • Stop for speech, a Rabbit Hole entry, a lost original, a mismatched result, 90 seconds unresolved, or the third semantic non-match (including a target whose transport degraded).
