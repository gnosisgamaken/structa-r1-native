# STRUCTA V3 product pilot

Run this before describing V3 as generally available. The purpose is to prove that the project instrument creates useful continuity—not merely plausible-looking output.

## Cohort

Use nine real, currently stuck projects:

| Lens | Projects | Minimum reference mix |
|---|---:|---|
| Vibe coding / digital product | 3 | Current-build screen, sketch or flow, external reference |
| Communication campaign | 3 | Rough concept, visual precedent, channel or placement context |
| Spatial / rebuilding | 3 | Sketch or plan, existing space, material or object |

The project owner must bring a real project and may not prepare a special STRUCTA brief in advance.

## Fifteen-minute session

1. Start from a cold project and let the participant describe it naturally.
2. Allow SHOW and TELL in any order. Do not coach wording or explain hidden schemas.
3. Stop at fifteen minutes, even if the participant wants to continue.
4. Ask the participant to inspect KNOW and NOW, then make only the corrections they believe matter.
5. Export both Markdown and `.structa.json`.

Record timestamps for first saved input, first usable map, first decision-ready NOW card, each correction, and export.

## Pass criteria per project

- The participant describes the map as materially correct after no more than three substantive edits.
- The participant agrees that NOW identifies the highest-leverage unresolved intervention.
- Original statements remain recognizable and are not silently rewritten as facts.
- Every visual reference has a truth role; uncertain roles or observations are batched for review.
- No decision locks without explicit Side/touch approval or a spoken custom answer.
- Back changes no decision or uncertainty.
- The project remains navigable after an injected offline/LLM failure.

## Blind handoff

Give the exported artifact to a collaborator or coding agent who did not attend the session. Do not provide chat history or let them contact the project owner for ten minutes.

The handoff passes when the recipient can state:

- the intended outcome and audience;
- the current direction and material constraints;
- what is evidence, proposal, external reference, or unresolved;
- which decisions are approved versus pending;
- the next useful action;
- one concrete contribution they can begin without re-interviewing the owner.

## Scoring

| Measure | Release threshold |
|---|---:|
| Clear map within 15 minutes | 8 / 9 projects |
| Correctable in ≤3 substantive edits | 8 / 9 |
| Highest-leverage NOW card accepted | 8 / 9 |
| Blind handoff succeeds | 8 / 9 |
| Claim/reference provenance correct | 9 / 9 |
| Silent decision changes or cross-project writes | 0 |
| Lost originals | 0 |

Any silent decision, cross-project write, lost original, or unsafe spatial conclusion is an automatic release blocker.

## Exit review

Classify findings as core-engine, interaction, domain-pack, vision-bridge, or documentation issues. Fix core-engine and interaction blockers before adding more packs. Promote repeated domain-specific patterns into declarative pack data only after they occur in at least three real projects.

Before public distribution, attach evidence that the bundled font may be embedded and redistributed, or replace it with a distribution-safe typeface.
